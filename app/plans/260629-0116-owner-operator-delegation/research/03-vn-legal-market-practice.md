# Vietnamese Legal Framework & Market Practice: Owner-Operator Delegation
**Research Date:** 2026-06-29  
**Status:** Research complete — **requires legal counsel review before product launch**

---

## Executive Summary

Vietnamese law permits property delegation through **hợp đồng ủy quyền** (power of attorney contract, Civil Code Arts. 138, 562) with written authorization typically acceptable for operational tasks, though **notarization (công chứng) is mandatory** for certain transactions. Temporary residence registration (KB04/CT01) has strict 12-hour deadline with joint owner/operator liability. Payment intermediary licensing (NHNN) thresholds unclear for ancu365's auto-transfer feature. Property management commission norms: **7–10% monthly + 0.5–1 month placement fee**. E-signatures valid under 2023 E-Transactions Law. **Co-ownership delegation remains ambiguous** — joint consent likely required for disposition, needs counsel verification.

## 1. ĐẠI DIỆN THEO UỶ QUYỀN — Legal Representation by Authorization

### 1.1 Civil Code Foundation (Bộ Luật Dân Sự 2015)

**Article 138: Authorization by Power of Attorney**  
- Individuals and legal entities can grant POA to others to establish and perform civil transactions (hợp đồng, giao dịch).  
- POA can be granted through a **hợp đồng ủy quyền** (power of attorney contract) or administrative document.  
- Authorization party must have **capacity to perform the type of civil transaction** they're authorizing.  
- Authorized party can be a minor aged 15–18 if the transaction permits it.

**Article 562: Commission Contract (Hợp đồng ủy quyền)**  
- Commission contract = agreement where commissioned agent performs work **on behalf of principal**, with principal paying compensation if agreed or required by law.  
- **Compensation is optional** (compensated vs. non-compensated).  
- If compensated: Principal must pay reasonable expenses + agreed-upon fee.  
- Principal can unilaterally terminate but must pay for work completed + damages.

**Source:** [Quy định về đại diện theo ủy quyền (thuvienphapluat.vn)](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/chinh-sach-phap-luat-moi/vn/ho-tro-phap-luat/tu-van-phap-luat/53138/quy-dinh-ve-dai-dien-theo-uy-quyen-trong-bo-luat-dan-su-2015)

### 1.2 Notarization Requirement (Công Chứng)

**Status: ESTABLISHED LAW**

- Property-related POAs **must be notarized** (công chứng) at a notary office to have full legal effect.  
- Notarization required for: signing/transferring red books (sổ đỏ), disposing of property, entering into binding contracts.  
- For **operational authorization only** (e.g., collecting rent, filing KB04): written authorization may be sufficient without notarization, though best practice is to notarize.  
- For **rental contract signature on owner's behalf**: notarized POA strongly recommended (verify with local notary office).

**Source:** [Thủ tục công chứng hợp đồng ủy quyền (vietnamembassy-usa.org)](https://vietnamembassy-usa.org/vi/basic-page/thu-tuc-cong-chung-hop-dong-giao-dich-uy-quyen-tai-san-hoac-di-san-thua-ke-va-chung-nhan), [Ủy Quyền là gì, POA, PD (vietnamtimemagazine.com)](https://vietnamtimemagazine.com/cac-hinh-thuc-uy-quyen.html)

### 1.3 PMC (Property Management Company) as Authorized Representative

**Status: ESTABLISHED LAW (with conditions)**

**From Real Estate Business Law 2023 (Luật Kinh Doanh Bất Động Sản 2023):**
- A registered real estate management company can act as operator/representative if:
  1. Holds valid **GPKD** (giấy phép kinh doanh — business license) with "quản lý bất động sản" (property management) in scope.
  2. Signs **hợp đồng dịch vụ quản lý** (property management service contract) with property owner.
  3. Performs work **with written authorization** from property owner.

- PMC can: collect rent, sign tenancy agreements (with owner's POA), file KB04, coordinate maintenance, transfer payments.
- PMC acts as **representative (đại diện)** or **agent (đại lý)** depending on contract terms.

**Individual Operator:**
- Individual property manager (not PMC company) can also be authorized as **hợp đồng ủy quyền** recipient.  
- Does NOT require business license for operational tasks (rent collection, KB04 filing).  
- **Likely requires** business registration (kinh doanh cá nhân) if taking commission; may face TNCN (personal income tax) at 17% on commission income.

**Source:** [Kinh doanh dịch vụ quản lý bất động sản (luatlongphan.vn)](https://luatlongphan.vn/dieu-kien-de-kinh-doanh-dich-vu-quan-ly-bat-dong-san), [Lĩnh vực Xây dựng — Kinh doanh bất động sản (dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn)](https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn/vn/Pages/ChitietNN.aspx?type=1&lvID=7&nnID=3395)

## 2. KB04 / CT01 — Temporary Residence Registration (Khai Báo Tạm Trú)

### 2.1 Timeline & Deadline (Luật Cư Trú 2020, Nghị Định 62/2021)

**Status: ESTABLISHED LAW**

- **Deadline: 12 hours** from when tenant moves in (or 24 hours for remote/mountainous areas).  
- For **renewal/extension**: must apply within **15 days before expiration** of current temporary residence registration.

**Form:** CT01 (formerly referred to as KB04)

**Source:** [Thủ tục đăng ký tạm trú (luatvietnam.vn)](https://luatvietnam.vn/hanh-chinh/thu-tuc-dang-ky-tam-tru-570-28204-article.html), [Luật Cư trú 2020 số 68/2020/QH14 (thuvienphapluat.vn)](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Quyen-dan-su/Luat-68-2020-QH14-cu-tru-435315.aspx)

### 2.2 Liability: Owner or Operator?

**Status: AMBIGUOUS — needs legal counsel**

**Current Law:**
- **Nghị định 62/2021, Article 5:** Property owner/host facility operator must sign form CT01 to **confirm** tenant has permission to reside at that address.
- **Owner's responsibility:** Tenant's temporary residence registration cannot be filed without owner's signature/approval.

**Ambiguity:**
- When operator files KB04 on owner's behalf: **Is operator liable for late filing, or owner?**
  - Law text suggests **owner is liable** (owner must authorize).
  - **Practical reality:** Cảnh sát quản lý hành chính (police) may fine **either or both** depending on local enforcement.
  - **For ancu365:** Recommend platform contract clearly allocate liability. Standard practice: **operator is responsible for timely filing**, owner reimburses any penalties.

### 2.3 Operator Authorization: Notarized POA or Written?

**Status: AMBIGUOUS**

- Luật Cư trú 2020 does not explicitly require POA to be notarized.  
- **Current practice (common, not law):** Written authorization + operator's signature + owner's phone verification often accepted by local police.
- **Best practice:** Notarized POA recommended to avoid disputes; cost ~300K–500K VND.
- **For ancu365:** Platform can support both notarized + unnotarized POA options; flag to owner that notarized = safer.

**Source:** [Nghị định 62/2021/NĐ-CP (thuvienphapluat.vn)](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Quyen-dan-su/Nghi-dinh-62-2021-ND-CP-chi-tiet-mot-so-dieu-luat-cu-tru-204481-d1.html)

### 2.4 Penalty for Late Filing

**Status: ESTABLISHED LAW**

- **Late/missing KB04 filing:** VND 500,000–1,000,000 fine for individual owner.  
- For **organizations:** 2x the individual fine (VND 1,000,000–2,000,000).
- Applies if filed after 12-hour deadline or not filed at all.

**Source:** [Mức phạt không đăng ký tạm trú (thuvienphapluat.vn)](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/chinh-sach-phap-luat-moi/vn/ho-tro-phap-luat/chinh-sach-moi/100164/muc-phat-khong-dang-ky-tam-tru-moi-nhat-2025-2026), [Mức phạt hành vi không thực hiện đúng quy định (xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn)](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/muc-phat-hanh-vi-khong-thuc-hien-dung-quy-dinh-cua-phap-luat-ve-dang-ky-tam-tru-119251104164040733.htm)

## 3. PDPL — Personal Data Protection (Nghị Định 13/2023)

### 3.1 Consent Framework

**Status: ESTABLISHED LAW (narrow interpretation)**

**Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP (effective 2023-07-01):**
- All personal data processing requires **explicit, affirmative consent** from data subject (individual).
- Consent can be given in writing, voice, checkbox, text message syntax, or technical settings.
- **Dual responsibility:** Both data controller and data processor must prove consent in dispute.

**Data Controller vs. Data Processor:**
- **Owner = Data Controller** (determines purpose/method of processing tenant/bank data).
- **Operator = Data Processor** (processes data on behalf of owner per contract).
- Processor contract must specify data types, storage period, security measures.

**Source:** [Decree No. 13/2023/ND-CP (thuvienphapluat.vn)](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/EN/Cong-nghe-thong-tin/Decree-No-13-2023-ND-CP-dated-April-17-2023-on-protection-of-personal-data/564343/tieng-anh.aspx), [Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Decree (securiti.ai)](https://securiti.ai/vietnam-personal-data-protection-decree/)

### 3.2 CCCD (Citizen ID) Data — Tenant Consent

**Status: LIKELY COVERED (but ambiguous)**

**Likely scenario:**
- When tenant signs rental contract with owner, consent form typically covers "landlord may collect/store CCCD for verification & legal compliance."  
- When operator views CCCD (to verify tenant identity, complete KB04): **probably covered by tenant's original consent to landlord**, not requiring separate consent to operator.

**Risk:**
- PDPL doesn't explicitly clarify whether tenant consent to owner = consent to operator.  
- **Best practice for ancu365:** Operator POA should include clause: "Operator may access tenant's CCCD, bank account, and contact info for property management purposes under landlord's authorization."

### 3.3 Owner Bank Account Data — Owner Consent

**Status: REQUIRES OWNER CONSENT (explicit)**

**Issue:** If ancu365 auto-transfers commission from owner's bank account:
- Owner's bank account number = **sensitive personal data** (financial record).
- Operator accessing/storing owner's bank details = **data processor** role.
- Owner must explicitly consent: "Operator may access my bank details to transfer commission."
- Consent scope must specify: transfer amount, frequency, duration.

**Implication for ancu365:**  
- If platform auto-transfers commission: **explicit written consent required** from owner per PDPL.
- If owner manually initiates transfer: no PDPL issue (owner controls access).

**Source:** [Consent Requirements (Vietnam Personal Data Protection Law)](https://secureprivacy.ai/blog/vietnam-personal-data-protection-law), [Sensitive Personal Data Definition](https://www.cookieyes.com/blog/vietnam-personal-data-protection-law/)

### 3.4 Data Storage & Encryption

**Status: ESTABLISHED LAW**

- Decree 13/2023 requires data **stored securely with encryption** (ngăn chặn truy cập trái phép).  
- Storage period must be **no longer than necessary** for stated purpose.  
- For KB04: CCCD data should be **deleted 6–12 months after tenant checkout** (common practice, not codified).

## 4. TAX TREATMENT OF OPERATOR COMMISSION

### 4.1 Individual Operator (Kinh doanh cá nhân)

**Status: ESTABLISHED LAW (2026 changes)**

**Personal Income Tax (TNCN):**
- Commission income taxed at **17% on (revenue – reasonable expenses)**.  
- Revenue threshold change (Jan 2026): operators earning ≥ VND 500M/year must declare TNCN (previously VND 100M).

**VAT:**
- Property management service = standard VAT (10%) or reduced VAT (5%, some regions).  
- Only applies if operator registered for VAT.  
- VAT exemption threshold raised to **VND 500M annual revenue** (Jan 2026, previously VND 100M).

**Invoice requirement (Hoá đơn):**
- If operator earns commission, should issue **hoá đơn** to owner (TT200/TT78 format).  
- Owner can deduct commission as business expense on own tax return.

**Withholding:**  
- No explicit withholding requirement; operator self-files TNCN monthly/quarterly.

**Source:** [New 2026 Rules for Household Enterprises Tax (vietanlaw.com)](https://vietanlaw.com/new-2026-rules-for-household-enterprises-tax-in-vietnam/), [Personal Income Tax in Vietnam (vietnam.acclime.com)](https://vietnam.acclime.com/guides/personal-income-tax/), [Property Rental Tax Obligations (tpm.com.vn)](https://tpm.com.vn/property-rental-tax-obligations/)

### 4.2 PMC Operator (Doanh nghiệp)

**Status: ESTABLISHED LAW**

**Corporate Income Tax (CIT):**
- PMC taxed at **20% CIT** on service income.  
- Income = commission from owner (not gross rent).

**VAT:**
- PMC must issue VAT invoice at **10% standard** (or 5% reduced for property management service in some regions).  
- VAT-registered PMC deducts input VAT on business expenses.

**Rental Income to Owner:**  
- Owner's rental income taxed **on gross rent (before commission deduction)**.  
- Commission is not deductible from owner's income tax.

**Example:** Owner collects VND 10M rent, pays operator 8% (VND 800K):
- Owner declares VND 10M as income (not VND 9.2M after commission).
- Operator declares VND 800K as service income, pays 20% CIT + 10% VAT.

**Source:** [Property Management Fees Vietnam 2026 (hausive.com)](https://www.hausive.com/blog/property-management-fees-vietnam/), [Vietnam's New VAT Law in 2026 (vietnam-briefing.com)](https://www.vietnam-briefing.com/news/vietnam-new-vat-law-key-compliance-guidance.html/)

## 5. NHNN PAYMENT INTERMEDIARY LICENSE (Trung Gian Thanh Toán)

### 5.1 Regulatory Framework

**Status: ESTABLISHED LAW (threshold unclear for ancu365's use case)**

**Circular 40/2024/TT-NHNN** (effective 2024, replaced Circular 39/2014):
- State Bank of Vietnam (NHNN) regulates non-bank intermediary payment services.  
- Intermediary payment includes: switching (chuyển mạch), electronic clearing, e-wallet, payment gateway services.  
- Licensed providers: VETC, MoMo, Payoo, Viettel Pay, etc. (see NHNN license list).

**Scope of intermediation:**
- Acting as intermediary between payer (owner) and payee (operator) for electronic fund transfer.  
- Providing payment gateway infrastructure (connecting owner's bank → operator's bank).

**Source:** [Circular 40/2024/TT-NHNN (NHNN State Bank)](https://sbv.gov.vn), [Thông tư 39/2014/TT-NHNN (thuvienphapluat.vn)](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Tien-te-Ngan-hang/Thong-tu-39-2014-TT-NHNN-huong-dan-dich-vu-trung-gian-thanh-toan-259960.aspx), [Intermediary Payment Services in Vietnam (vision-associates.com)](https://vision-associates.com/highlights-of-the-new-circular-on-intermediary-payment-services/)

### 5.2 Does ancu365 Need a Trung Gian License?

**Status: AMBIGUOUS — needs NHNN legal opinion**

**Scenario 1: ancu365 provides info/schedule, owner manually transfers**
- Owner uses their own banking app to initiate transfer to operator.  
- ancu365 is NOT intermediary = **no NHNN license needed**.

**Scenario 2: ancu365 auto-transfers (API integration with bank/MoMo)**
- ancu365 connects owner's bank account (or e-wallet) to operator's bank account.  
- ancu365 facilitates automatic fund flow = **likely = trung gian thanh toán**.  
- **May require NHNN license or partnership** with licensed intermediary.

**Current practice:**
- Platforms like MoMo, Payoo hold licenses; ancu365 can integrate via their API.  
- ancu365 would not own the license, but would integrate licensed provider's services.

**Recommendation for ancu365:**  
- **Do NOT auto-transfer funds directly**; instead, use VietQR/MoMo/Payoo integration (they hold licenses).  
- Or: provide scheduled transfer info (time, amount), let owner authorize each transfer manually.  
- **Escalate to NHNN legal team** if platform later wants to offer auto-settlement.

**Source:** [List of Licensed Payment Intermediary Providers (NHNN, Dec 2024)](https://sbv.gov.vn/en/w/sbv620432)

## 6. HỢP ĐỒNG DỊCH VỤ QUẢN LÝ — Property Management Service Contract

### 6.1 Standard Contract Elements (Market Practice)

**Status: ESTABLISHED PRACTICE (not codified in law)**

**Typical Clauses:**
1. **Parties:** Owner (chủ nhà), PMC/operator (quản lý viên).  
2. **Scope:** Rent collection, tenant screening, maintenance coordination, KB04 filing, etc.  
3. **Commission:** 6–10% of monthly rent + 0.5–1 month placement fee per new tenant.  
4. **Term:** 6 months, 1 year, or 3 years (common practice).  
5. **Termination notice:** 30–60 days written notice (standard).  
6. **Liability cap:** PMC not liable for tenant non-payment beyond reasonable effort to collect.  
7. **Expense threshold:** Maintenance repairs below VND 2M–5M approved by operator without owner sign-off; above = owner approval required.  
8. **Payment method:** Bank transfer only (no cash); operator can request VAT invoice format.

**Source:** [Property Management Fees Vietnam 2026: Benchmarks & Contract Guide (hausive.com)](https://www.hausive.com/blog/property-management-fees-vietnam/)

### 6.2 E-Signature Validity (Luật Giao Dịch Điện Tử 2023)

**Status: ESTABLISHED LAW**

**Law 20/2023/QH15** (E-Transactions Law, effective 2024-07-01):
- **E-signature (chữ ký điện tử)** = legally equivalent to handwritten signature if:
  1. **Specialized e-signature** (dùng nội bộ) = not valid for public transactions.  
  2. **Digital signature (chữ ký số)** = secured by digital certificate from licensed provider = VALID.  
  3. Public e-signature on government services = VALID.

**For ancu365 contracts:**
- Property management contract can be signed with **digital signature** (chữ ký số from FastCA, VinaCA, etc.) = legal.  
- Simpler e-signature (e.g., email confirmation, checkbox ticking) = risky, not clearly covered by law.

**Recommendation:** ancu365 should integrate certified digital signature provider (FastCA, VinaCA, VeriSign) for operator POA + contract.

**Source:** [Luật Giao dịch điện tử 2023 số 20/2023/QH15 (thuvienphapluat.vn)](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Cong-nghe-thong-tin/Luat-Giao-dich-dien-tu-2023-20-2023-QH15-513347.aspx), [New Regulations on E-Signatures (lexology.com)](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=436bcecc-6a92-4114-9cab-1b4ec2ccb260)

## 9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION & FRAUD PATTERNS

### 9.1 Fraud Risks in VN Real Estate (2024–2025)

**Status: REAL-WORLD PATTERNS (not codified)**

**Common Schemes:**
- **Ghost landlord:** Scammer rents property they don't own (fake POA).  
- **Bait-and-switch:** Operator lists property, collects deposit, disappears.  
- **Inflated Ký Gửi fees:** Multiple agents list same unit, drive up prices.  
- **Fake contracts:** Forged tenant agreements, fake CCCD copies.

**Incident Rate:**  
- **72% of expats report rental fraud attempts** in 2024 (VietRent survey).  
- **37% of expats encountered fraudulent agreements or fake registration papers** annually.

**Corporate Fraud (Notable Case):**  
- **Truong My Lan** (real estate tycoon): sentenced to death in April 2024 for $27B fraud (controlling Saigon JSC Bank illegally, 2012–2022). Appeal in Nov 2024.  
- **Not a property operator** fraud case, but shows VN has active real estate fraud ecosystem.

**Source:** [Vietnam Rental Scams: Complete Guide (viethomerental.com)](https://www.viethomerental.com/en/blogs/vietnam-rental-scams-complete-guide-to-avoiding-property-vehicle-fraud), [Vietnam sentences real estate tycoon Truong My Lan (npr.org)](https://www.npr.org/2024/04/12/1244263170/vietnam-tycoon-death-sentence)

### 9.2 Legal Recourse for Owner if Operator Absconds

**Status: LIMITED (enforcement weak)**

**Theory (Law):**
- Owner can sue operator for breach of contract (vi phạm hợp đồng) in People's Court.  
- Seek: damages (compensation), return of collected rent, contract termination.  
- Operator liable if: failed to remit rent, embezzled funds, forged documents.

**Practice (Reality):**
- VN court cases on operator fraud are rare in public record.  
- **Enforcement of court judgment:** slow (6 months–2 years).  
- Many operators are individuals with limited assets; judgment recovery = difficult.

**Insurance:**  
- **Property management liability insurance** is not widely available in VN as of 2026.  
- Some international PMCs offer fidelity bonds (khấu trừ).

**Recommendation for ancu365:**
- Operator screening: verify CCCD, business license (if PMC), background checks.  
- Escrow account: platform can hold/release rent in tranches (platform = trusted intermediary).  
- KYC checks: require business license + tax ID for high-value delegations.

**Source:** [Breaking the laws of the land: Vietnam's real estate scandals (rfa.org)](https://www.rfa.org/english/commentaries/vietnam-land-corruption-12222023132815.html)

## 10. CO-OWNERSHIP REALITY & DELEGATION

### 10.1 Common Scenarios in Vietnam

**Status: ESTABLISHED PRACTICE**

- **Spouse co-ownership:** Very common (husband + wife co-own rental property).  
- **Sibling co-ownership:** Less common but occurs (shared inheritance, family investment).  
- **Parent + adult child:** Occurs (parent transfers ownership share to child for tax/legal reasons).

### 10.2 Can One Co-Owner Unilaterally Delegate to Operator?

**Status: AMBIGUOUS — Needs counsel**

**Theory (Law):**
- **Ownership by shares (hợp đồng chia tách):** Each co-owner has defined % share.  
  - Disposition (selling, leasing, delegating management) of co-owned property requires **consent of all co-owners** (Article 212, Civil Code 2015).  
  - **One co-owner alone cannot delegate** to operator without other co-owners' approval.

- **Consolidated co-ownership (chung chủ):** Common property, shares not defined (typical for spouses).  
  - Same rule: **all parties must agree** on delegation or use of common property.  
  - Spouse can delegate with other spouse's permission; unilateral delegation = breach of family rights.

**Practice (Reality):**
- Many properties registered in one spouse's name (not jointly), circumventing co-ownership issue.  
- Some operators do not ask; if co-owner complains later, dispute arises.

**For ancu365:**  
- **Important disclosure:** Platform should require owner to confirm: "Is this property in your sole name, or co-owned? If co-owned, do all co-owners consent?"  
- If co-owned: require POA from **all co-owners**, not just one.  
- Document consent in delegation contract to mitigate liability.

   - [ ] Obtain written opinion from VN corporate lawyer on: co-owner liability, operator POA notarization requirement, NHNN intermediary status.
   - [ ] Clarify with Bộ Công An (Ministry of Public Security) or local police on KB04 filing by operator — who is liable if late?

2. **Operator POA template:**
   - Provide bilingual (Vietnamese + English) POA template in platform.  
   - Require owner to confirm: sole owner or co-owned? If co-owned, all co-owners must sign.  
   - Option 1: in-app digital signature (FastCA API integration).  
   - Option 2: downloadable template + manual notarization.

3. **Commission data handling:**
   - **Do NOT auto-transfer** owner's funds without explicit PDPL consent.  
   - Design: owner schedules transfer, operator requests payment, owner approves manually.  
   - Alternative: integrate VietQR/MoMo (licensed intermediary) for semi-automated transfer.

4. **KB04 filing workflow:**
   - Operator enters tenant details, owner reviews + approves digitally.  
   - Platform flags: "12-hour deadline — ensure submission to police within this window."  
   - Contract clause: "Operator responsible for timely KB04 filing; owner may reimburse fines if operator at fault."

## Unresolved Questions (Requires Lawyer Input)

1. **Does operator filing KB04 require notarized POA, or is written authorization sufficient?**  
   - Luật Cư trú 2020 silent on this; local police enforcement varies by province.

2. **If KB04 filed late: is owner liable, or operator, or both?**  
   - Law suggests owner is liable (owner must authorize); practice may differ.

3. **PDPL consent for operator viewing tenant CCCD: does tenant's original consent to owner = consent to operator, or separate consent required?**  
   - Decree 13/2023 doesn't explicitly address delegation context.

4. **If ancu365 provides transfer schedule (time + amount) but owner manually initiates bank transfer, does NHNN license apply?**  
   - Circular 40/2024/TT-NHNN doesn't define "acting as intermediary" in this gray zone.

5. **For co-owned property: must ALL co-owners sign delegation POA, or can one co-owner delegate their share?**  
   - Civil Code suggests all parties consent for disposition; "management" vs. "disposition" distinction unclear.

6. **Is property management service VAT taxed at 10% (standard) or 5% (reduced rate for services)?**  
   - Practice varies by province/HCMC tax department interpretation.
